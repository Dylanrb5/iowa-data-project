# Data-Quality Log

Issues found while cleaning the Iowa liquor sales data, with counts and decisions. Percentages are of the raw table (`raw.liquor_sales`: 26,500,494 rows, January 2017 – October 2026) unless stated otherwise. `staging.sales` keeps every raw row with flags added; `staging.sales_unique`, which all marts read, has 24,579,716 rows, exactly 26,500,494 minus the 1,920,778 duplicates in DQ-11.

| ID | Issue | Found by | Rows (% of total) | Decision | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DQ-01 | Store location fields missing together (address, city, ZIP, county) | Null profile | ~58,250 rows (0.22%) | Fill from the same store's other rows in `staging.stores`; exclude any remainder from county analysis | All stores filled except the 3 with no Iowa location anywhere (DQ-12) |
| DQ-02 | Category missing (8,751 of 8,777 rows in 2017–2020; 26 rows in 2026) | Null profile | 8,777 rows (0.03%) | Map to "(missing)" in `category_map` and the Other family; keep in totals, exclude from category analysis | 74,872 liters (under 0.05% of volume) left out of category analysis |
| DQ-03 | Vendor missing | Null profile | 7 rows (<0.001%) | No action; vendor isn't used in the analysis | None |
| DQ-04 | State bottle cost and retail price missing | Null profile | 22,676,660 rows (85.6%) | Not used; left out of staging. Dollars and liters come from `sales_dollars` and `sales_liters` | None |
| DQ-05 | Four category labels changed from plural to singular starting July 2025 (cordials & liqueurs; distilled spirits specialty; American and imported) | Label first/last year query | 8 labels, 1,390,257 rows (5.2%) | Merge each pair into one clean category in `seeds/category_map.csv` | Without the merge, these categories would show a false drop in 2025–26 |
| DQ-06 | Stray labels used in only a few years | Label first/last year query | 8 labels, 3,008 rows (0.01%) | Map to the nearest family where obvious (the whisky and gin labels); the rest go to Other | Negligible |
| DQ-07 | Non-spirit and catch-all categories (`TEMPORARY & SPECIALTY PACKAGES`, `SPECIAL ORDER ITEMS`) | Label first/last year query | 611,011 rows (2.3%) | Keep in totals under the Other family; exclude Other from category growth and mix-gap analysis | Category shares are measured against named spirit families; see DQ-15 for what these categories contain |
| DQ-08 | Store numbers with cosmetic name variants (typos, legal suffixes like LLC/ET AL, punctuation; same address and city) | Name-count query, then rule-based classification (name similarity / prefix match) with manual review of borderline cases | >64 of 3,132 stores (2%) | Join on `store_number`, never on name; `staging.stores` keeps the most recent name | None: names are cosmetic and don't affect joins or totals |
| DQ-09 | Store rebrands (e.g., Kum & Go to Maverik): old name ends, a different name begins under the same store number | Same classification; non-overlapping name periods with low name similarity, manually confirmed | <256 of 3,132 stores (8.2%) | Same store number kept; both brand names added to `store_type_rules.csv` | Store type stays stable across the rebrand; sales history is continuous under one store |
| DQ-10 | Same store reported under more than one county | Store-county conflict query (stores with >1 distinct county) | 17 stores, 20,822 rows (0.08%) | Set each store's county and FIPS to its most common value across all rows | Minority-county rows move to the store's majority county; about $2.65M of sales (counted before de-duplication) moves between counties |
| DQ-11 | Exact duplicate sales rows (identical in all 23 columns), concentrated in 2020–22 and mid-2025 onward | Row-identity query; fiscal-year totals vs ABD's published FY2021 sales ($415.8M) | 1,920,778 rows (7.2%) | Flagged (`flag_duplicate_row`), not deleted; marts read `staging.sales_unique` | Removes ~$321M (7.6% of raw sales); FY2021 goes from $500.8M to $417.1M, within 0.3% of the published figure (FY2022 is 2.1% below published after de-duplication) |
| DQ-12 | Stores with no Iowa location: out-of-state ZIP/FIPS or location null in every row | ZIP and FIPS checked against Iowa codes | 3 stores, 5,684 liters (<0.01%) | Flagged (`has_iowa_county = false`); kept in statewide totals, excluded from county and per-adult analysis | Negligible |
| DQ-13 | `invoice_id` is not a unique line ID, and its grain varies by year (how many lines share one ID changes over time) | Distinct-count checks by year; a first de-duplication on `invoice_id` removed far too much | About 4.66M rows would have been dropped by an `invoice_id` de-duplication | Added a surrogate key (`sale_row_id`); duplicates are judged on all 23 columns (DQ-11), never on `invoice_id` alone | Avoids losing about 18% of rows that are legitimate separate lines |
| DQ-14 | 2026 is a partial year (January – October at download) | Rows by month | About 2M rows (~7.5%) | Annual analyses use 2017–2025 only (growth: 2019 → 2025); 2026 kept for the monthly COVID series | Prevents a false 2026 "decline" in yearly figures |
| DQ-15 | Products filed outside their natural family: Crown Royal flavors under Temporary & Specialty Packages, budget agave spirits (Tortilla Gold, Juarez Gold) under Distilled Spirits Specialty, Member's Mark vodka under Special Order Items | Item-level breakdown of what grew in "Other" | Agave items about 145,000 L in 2025 (~14% of tequila volume); Crown Royal flavors about 136,000 L (~3% of whiskey) | Kept as filed; impact quantified in findings (A1). An item-level override seed is a planned fix | "Other" growth is inflated; tequila growth is likely understated (headline is conservative) and whiskey's decline slightly overstated |
| DQ-16 | Canned cocktails (≤15% ABV) are distributed as beer in Iowa since 2019, and malt- and wine-based RTDs were never state-sold, so they're absent from this data | Category profile (average RTD bottle 1.3–1.6 L) and Iowa law | Coverage gap, not rows | RTD/Cocktails reported as "spirits-based premixes sold through the state" only | National RTD growth is not visible here; RTD results can't be compared with national figures |
| DQ-17 | Monthly totals swing about ±10% with the calendar (dates are ABD invoice dates; the number of ordering days per month varies, and the baseline has only two of each month), giving recurring March and September peaks | Monthly COVID index outside the restriction period | All months | Read runs of months, not single months; quarterly or 3-month views in the dashboard | Single-month index values carry about ±10 points of noise |
| DQ-18 | Store ZIPs with no matching Census ZCTA: transposed or mistyped ZIPs and non-street ZIPs | Direct ZIP→ZCTA match rate | 9 stores, about 545K liters (0.24%) before fixes | ZIPs corrected by hand where the address confirms it (stores 2687, 5091, 5054, 4204, 4864, 5142); no ZIP crosswalk used | All stores with an Iowa location matched; only the 3 DQ-12 stores (5,684 liters) lack ZIP demographics |
| DQ-19 | Store type derived from name rules; names that match no rule fall to `other` | Coverage by type after loading `store_type_rules.csv` | `other` = 7% of liters (target under 10%) | Rules matched in priority order: specific chains first, then liquor words, then generic convenience and grocery words | Store type is an estimate. Judgment calls: Central City, Another Round and Smokin' Joe's as liquor stores; Benz Distributing as wholesale |
| DQ-20 | `sales_liters` is 0 on rows with valid bottle size and count, all in 2025–26 | Volume-flag breakdown by year and kind | 140,355 rows (0.57%) | Liters derived as `bottle_volume_ml × sales_bottles / 1000`; marked `liters_derived` | Restores 2025–26 volume that would otherwise drop out of liter-based marts; no rows left with bad volume |
| DQ-21 | Negative quantities and liters (returns and credits), 2022 onward | Same breakdown | 11,763 rows (0.048%) | Kept in totals and flagged `flag_return`; not treated as bad data | Totals are net of returns, consistent with published dollar figures |
| DQ-22 | Rows with zero or missing bottle quantity | Flag counts on `staging.sales_unique` | 3 rows (<0.001%) | Flagged `flag_bad_qty`; excluded from the bottle-based monthly mart | Negligible |
| DQ-23 | Some high-volume stores buy like bar and restaurant suppliers (mostly 1 L bottles, few minis or handles); name-based store types miss this (e.g., Hy-Vee #3 / BDI is classified as grocery) | Bottle-size mix by store | 15 stores, about 11% of statewide liters | Flagged `supplier_profile` (≥60% 1 L bottles, <10% handles, <10% minis, ≥20,000 L per active year); per-adult and growth results reported with and without them; excluded from peer benchmarking | Volume bought in one county but poured elsewhere no longer inflates that county (Clinton drops from 2nd to 41st in liters per adult). At a 50% cutoff the headline counties are unchanged except Johnson County (Dickinson, Scott and Dubuque shares also shift; see A7) |
| DQ-24 | Some mini (≤200 ml) items are multi-packs (sleeves, dispensers) recorded with the single-mini volume, so their liters are understated about 10–20× and price per liter is inflated | Price per unit far above single-mini prices (e.g., $18–39 per "50 ml bottle") | 14.4% of 2025 rows; 7.1% of 2025 dollars ($30.2M) | Flagged `flag_multipack`; price per liter computed without them; peer benchmarking and category gaps use dollars | Statewide liters understated by roughly 3–5%; much more for mini-heavy stores (mainly convenience) |
